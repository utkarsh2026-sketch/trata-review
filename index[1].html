About PROJECT TRĀTA

TRĀTA is an autonomous disaster-response concept designed to support safer and faster emergency operations. The system combines a rescue rover, aerial monitoring, sensors and a control interface to help inspect difficult or unsafe areas and assist responders with situational information.
